What is digital marketing?
• “Digital marketing” is the process of building and maintaining customer
relationships through online activities to facilitate the exchange of ideas,
products, and services that satisfy the goals of both parties.
• The objective of marketing is to reach potential customers through the
channels where they already spend their time reading, searching, shopping
and socializing online.

Pay Per Click (PPC)
• Pay per click (PPC) is a type of sponsored online advertising that is used on a
wide range of websites, including search engines, where the advertiser only
pays if a web user clicks on their ad. Hence the title, 'pay per click'.
• Advertisers place bids on keywords or phrases that they think their target
audience would type in a search field when they are looking for specific goods
or services.

What is Social media marketing (SMM)?
• Social Media Marketing (SMM) is a
form of digital marketing that utilizes
social media platforms to promote
products, services, or brands.
• The primary goal is to connect with
the target audience, build brand
awareness, drive website traffic, and
increase sales or other desired
actions.
<!-- p17 -->
Basic social media marketing (SMM) strategy
• The POST method is an excellent starting point for any SMM campaign.
• The POST method stands for People, Objectives, Strategy, Technology, and this
method helps to address the burning question of should my company have a social
media marketing strategy?
• POST Method:
✓People: Do you know who your target audience is? What are their demographics
and interests?
✓Objectives: What do you want to accomplish with your social media marketing
campaign?
✓Strategy: How will things be different after you launch your social media
marketing campaign?
✓Technology: What social media channels are you going to use?

Online Advertisement
Any marketing message that appears online is referred to as online advertising. In other
words, it might show up in a web browser, search engine, social media platform, mobile
device, or even email.
For a variety of reasons, savvy advertisers are increasingly using this venue to connect with
consumers.
• It reaches a big audience and is reasonably priced
• It can be customised for a target demographic and tracked to gauge success (or failure).
<!-- p26 -->
Types of Online Advertising
✓ Banner Ad:
A banner advertises a good or service with some brief text or visuals. A website object is a banner advertisement. It offers a
link to the advertiser's website; banner ads enable users to access the advertiser's website whenever they click them; they
simultaneously inform and compel customers to purchase the advertised goods.
✓ Pop-up Ad:
When a person opens or closes a web page, a pop-up advertisement shows in a separate window. Pop-up adverts are typically
thought to be quite obnoxious and irritating. These pop-up adverts can be blocked using the browser's built-in options.
✓ Ads that Interrupt:
After clicking a link to a new online page, an interstitial ad appears in the user's own browser window. Before the targeted
web page loads, the advertisement is displayed for a little while. Many interstitial advertisements shut down immediately,
allowing the desired page to load in the current browser window.
✓ Discounts:
Some businesses provide their customers with coupons for discounts that they can print and use on both online and offline
retainers. Coupons help customers to shop on a budget and take advantage of sales. It is simple for marketers to determine
the kinds of things a customer is interested in buying when they click on specific coupons. The vouchers give advertisers a
way to keep an eye on consumer behaviour. Online coupons inspire people since they enable them to save money. For
instance, coolsavings.com offers online coupons from well-known department shops and online retailers, saving customers'
time and money when they shop online. They provide coupons for things like beauty goods, snacks, groceries, apparel, travel,
health, and education.

✓ A reward system:
Customers who make online purchases receive reward points. Once the reward points have been acquired, they can be used to buy things
in the future. Offering reward points is intended to entice customers to spend a lot of money so they can accumulate more reward points
and benefit from additional rewards. For instance, Taj Hotels offers a variety of unique perks to clients who utilise their services globally,
including the opportunity to earn reward points. For important business bookers, Taj hotels have another programme. Making
reservations at the Taj entitles you to reward points and exclusive Taj deals.
✓ Pay per view of advertising:
Businesses reward consumers for viewing their adverts. Think of Cyber Gold.
✓ Changing the Banner:
Direct link exchanges between websites are involved. It's possible for "A" firm to agree to link to company "Bbanner, which will then link
to company "Abanner.
✓ Monitoring:
By installing small files known as "Cookies" on the viewer's computer, it is possible to monitor the response of the advertisement or
email's recipient. Receiving information on the types of consumers who are reacting to various offers and learning the kinds of offers that
normally get a positive response are both helpful to advertising
✓ E-mail :
Email can inexpensively and globally reach a large audience. Customers react negatively to unsolicited email, but they welcome solicited
email. Email advertising has many benefits, including massive reach, an audience that is remarkably well-educated and wealthy,
unmatched targeting, real-time tracking, rich media branding, lead generation, and direct sales.
